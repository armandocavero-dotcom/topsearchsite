Executive Search

Executive search is a business consulting practice whose purpose is to connect organizations with their future leaders, pursuing the success of both parties.

The way your consultant serves as “your ambassador” before candidates and carries out the contracted service is something inherently personal, related to their style, interpersonal skills, professional level, and above all, their ethics—a quality especially important in this environment.

At Top Search Peru, members of InterSearch Worldwide, you will find the professional experience and personal qualities that your company requires and deserves, to entrust this important role regarding your brand and image before the professionals you wish to attract.

Executive search FAQ

How long does an executive search take?

In our Head Hunting in the Cloud® process, the Long List is presented in week 4 and the Finalist Report in week 6. Market research continues until you choose your candidate.

What is the difference between executive search and a recruitment agency?

Executive search is business consulting: we research the market and approach executives who are not necessarily looking for a job, with a senior consultant acting as an ambassador for your brand. Traditional recruitment usually works with applicants and databases.

How does a search start?

With the presentation and approval of the Search Strategy, which defines the profile, the target market and the timeline, together with the first payment.

What guarantee do you offer?

A guarantee of up to 2 years — the market standard is 1 year — whose length depends on the level of the search.

Who leads the search?

Always a partner of the firm: former multinational CEOs and company directors who personally interview the Short List and conduct the references.

Do you run searches outside Peru?

Yes. As the exclusive InterSearch Worldwide member in Peru, we coordinate searches with partners in more than 90 offices worldwide.
