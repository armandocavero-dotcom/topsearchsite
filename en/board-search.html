Board Search

Board member search is a relatively recent practice in the world of executive search. The growing awareness of the need to improve corporate governance has contributed to the development of this new area of business consulting.

If you want your consultant to truly understand your needs and competently assess the suitability of your new directors, it is essential that they have served on a board themselves, so they can understand the competencies and experience required of the candidates to be presented.

The seniority of your consultant will be the differentiating and added value that brings the greatest depth and rigor to this practice.

Independent directors for your board

We search for independent directors, board members and advisory board members for family businesses, corporations and multinationals in Peru. Board search FAQ

What is an independent director?

A board member with no ties to management, controlling shareholders or related parties, who brings objective judgment to the company’s decisions.

Why hire a firm to find directors?

Because it extends the search beyond the shareholders’ network, provides an objective assessment of each candidate and keeps the process confidential.

Do you also search for advisory board members?

Yes. We search for directors, board members and advisory board members.

Who leads these searches?

A partner who serves or has served on boards, because assessing a director requires having been part of a board.
